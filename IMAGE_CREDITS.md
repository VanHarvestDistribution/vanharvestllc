## Summary of Optimization Standards
1. **Ratio Consistency**: All 9 department category cards are uniformly centered and cropped to 4:3.
2. **Format**: High-efficiency WebP and AVIF formats generated across 400w, 800w, and 1200w viewport breakpoints.
3. **No Synthetic Imagery**: Zero AI-generated portraits, food graphics, or clip art are used across the entire public website.
